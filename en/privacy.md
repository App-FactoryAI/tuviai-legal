# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is issued by ZiWei AI ("ZiWei AI", "we" or "us") to help Users understand what data we collect, how we use and share it, and the measures we take to protect personal data when you use the ZiWei AI mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy applies to everyone who has used or is using the App, and forms an integral part of the ZiWei AI [Terms of Service](../terms/).

ZiWei AI respects the privacy of every individual. We only collect the data needed to provide our services, and only after you agree to the contents of this Policy.

## 1. Scope and consent

1.1. When you use the App, we need your consent to this Privacy Policy (including any future amended versions). Consent is given through actions such as: signing in or creating an account, tapping "Agree" or "Continue" in the App, entering details to create a birth chart, or continuing to use the App (each an "Act of Consent").

1.2. By performing an Act of Consent, you confirm that you have read, understood and agree to the collection and processing of data under this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not perform any Act of Consent and stop using the App. If you decline to provide certain information, some features of the App may not work fully.

## 2. Data collected and why

### 2.1. Data we collect

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email, display name, profile photo (when signing in with email, Google or Apple) and account ID | Creating and managing your account, signing in, support |
| Name, birth date and hour, gender and calendar type (lunar/solar) of each birth chart | Building birth charts, generating readings and saving charts for later |
| Conversations with the Assistant (questions and replies) | Answering your questions and keeping your chat history for you to review |
| Transaction details (package purchased, transaction ID, status, subscription period) | Verifying purchases, crediting Coins and benefits, restoring purchases, Coin usage history |
| Push notification token, operating system, device model, App version | Sending notifications (e.g. Daily Fortune reminders, lunar holidays), technical support and keeping the App stable |
| Device language and the language you choose | Showing the App and readings in the right language |
| Permission to save images to your photo library (write-only, no reading) | Saving your birth chart image to your device when you ask |

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store your card or payment account details.

### 2.2. Data we do not collect

The App does **not** collect your location or contacts, does not access your camera or read your photo library, does not use cookies, does not collect advertising identifiers and does not track you across other apps or websites.

### 2.3. Personal data of other people

You may create birth charts for other people (family, friends, etc.). In that case, you must make sure you have that person's consent before entering their information into the App. ZiWei AI is not liable for any third-party claim relating to your use of their personal data without permission.

## 3. AI processing

To generate readings and Assistant replies, the App sends the necessary data to our artificial intelligence provider, including: chart information (birth date and hour, gender, calendar type, stars and palaces on the chart), the display language and the questions you send to the Assistant.

We do not send your email, sign-in details or payment information to the AI provider. The AI provider may process data on servers located in a country other than the one where you live.

You may choose not to use AI readings and the Assistant if you do not want this data sent to the AI provider. By using these features, you agree to the data processing described in this Section.

## 4. Sharing personal data

ZiWei AI **does not sell** your personal data and **does not share** it with advertising partners. We only share data in the following cases:

4.1. **Service providers** that help operate the App, and only to the extent necessary to provide our services to you:

- Providers of server infrastructure, databases and sign-in services;
- Providers of in-app purchase verification;
- Providers of push notification delivery;
- Providers of artificial intelligence services (as described in Section 3);
- Apple and Google, as the platforms for app distribution, payment processing and sign-in.

4.2. **When required by law:** where necessary to comply with legal obligations or lawful requests from competent authorities, or to protect the rights, property and safety of ZiWei AI, our Users or others.

4.3. **Business transfers:** if ZiWei AI is merged, acquired or transfers all or part of its operations, personal data may be transferred to the receiving party, provided that it continues to protect the data in line with this Policy.

## 5. International data transfers

The App is offered worldwide. Our servers and those of our service providers may be located in countries other than the one where you live. By using the App, you agree that your personal data may be transferred to and processed in those countries. We take reasonable measures to keep data safe during transfer.

## 6. Storage, security and deletion

6.1. Your personal data is stored on our infrastructure provider's cloud platform, with access controls so that each User can only access their own data. Data is encrypted in transit between the App and our servers.

6.2. We keep personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services to you, or longer if required by law.

6.3. **Account deletion requests:** to delete your account and personal data, please email **tuvigpt@gmail.com** from your registered account email with the subject **"Account deletion request"**. We will process the request within **30 days** of receiving a valid request.

6.4. When your account is deleted, we delete your account information, saved charts, readings and Assistant chat history. Some transaction data may be retained for as long as required by law or by Apple's and Google's policies. Remaining Coins, usage credits and benefits in the account are cancelled in accordance with the Terms of Service.

6.5. Deleting your account does **not** automatically cancel an auto-renewing subscription. You need to cancel it yourself in your App Store or Google Play settings.

## 7. Your rights

7.1. Depending on the laws of the place where you live, you have the following rights over your personal data: the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to delete, to withdraw consent, to restrict or object to processing, to request a copy of your data, and to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

7.2. You can view and edit some information (such as saved charts) directly in the App, or contact us using the details in Section 10 to exercise these rights. To protect your account, we may ask for additional information to verify that the requester is the account owner.

7.3. You can turn notification and photo library permissions on or off at any time in your device settings.

7.4. When you withdraw consent, restrict, object to processing or request deletion, we will stop processing or delete the relevant data. This may mean you can no longer use some or all features of the App.

## 8. Children's data

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children without the consent of a parent or legal guardian. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's online activities.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal data to the App without consent, please contact us using the details in Section 10 and we will delete that data.

## 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

ZiWei AI may update or amend this Privacy Policy at any time. Amended versions will be published and announced in the App. Continuing to use the App after an amended version takes effect means you accept it. Please check notices in the App regularly to stay informed of the latest updates.
